Our story

A coconut, a wood-burning pen, a really good idea.

Sigfruit started in a backyard in Trelawny with a hot iron and a handful of jelly coconuts. We wanted to make a gift that felt like Jamaica — warm, vivid, generous — without feeling like a souvenir.

Five years and a few thousand coconuts later, we still burn every order by hand. From single love-notes to 500-unit corporate drops, the standard is the same: if it's not photo-ready, we don't ship it.
